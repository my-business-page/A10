## Site map
Home / Coffee Menu / Beans & Roasts / Bulk Orders / Events & Weddings / About / Order / Pickup / FAQ / Contact.

## Conversion strategy
The site should sell both individual cups and higher-value B2B/event enquiries. Home hero: specialty coffee roasted in Singapore. Secondary CTA: “Plan Coffee for an Event”.

## Visual
Modern roastery minimalism: charcoal, parchment, stainless-steel/silver, espresso brown. Grid-heavy editorial design with macro roast/bean imagery.

## Motion/VFX
- Hero coffee-bean particle field, extremely subtle.
- Scroll-based roast-stage storytelling.
- Product cards reveal roast notes as the card rotates 2–4 degrees.
- Event packages animate as stacked cards.
- Scroll entrance/exit fade + blur + translate.
- Small steam/shadow VFX, no excessive 3D.

## Functional
Bean/menu data model; event enquiry form; bulk-order form; Instagram DM link; order/pickup CTA; roast notes; FAQs; accessibility; SEO; responsive image optimization.

## Accuracy
Exact address, operating hours, phone, Instagram handle, prices, order platform and current availability must be verified before launch. Never fabricate roast origins or certifications.
